## 📖 About The Project

This repository serves as a centralized hub for tracking and sharing interview questions specifically tailored for DevOps and DevSecOps roles. Whether you are aiming for IT service giants like **TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, HCL**, or other organizations, you'll find real-world questions categorized by topic and company to aid in your preparation.

### 🎯 Key Technologies Covered
* **Cloud & Infrastructure:** Azure, Terraform
* **Containers & Orchestration:** Docker, Kubernetes
* **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions, Azure Pipelines
* **DevSecOps & Security Tools:** tflint, tfsec, gitleaks

## 🤝 How to Contribute

We welcome contributions! If you recently had a DevOps or DevSecOps interview and want to share your experience (without violating NDAs), please follow these steps:

1. **Fork** the repository.
2. **Clone** your fork locally.
3. **Navigate** to the specific company folder (e.g., `companies/TCS/`) or topic (e.g., `topics/Terraform.md`).
4. **Add** your questions.
5. **Commit** your changes and open a **Pull Request**.

Please read our [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for more detailed guidelines.
