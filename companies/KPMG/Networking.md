MFA Pop-up: When you log on to a Windows Server, do you get a pop-up to use MFA?

Secure Connection Methods: If you connect to a virtual machine in your subscription (e.g., via RDP or other options), how do you secure that form of connection? Are you controlling access using another tool (e.g., Bastion)?

Bastion Architecture: Do you have Bastion in your hub-and-spoke setup, or are you using a single Bastion in your current organization?
