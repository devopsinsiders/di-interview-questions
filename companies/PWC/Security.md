# Security Questions at PWC

Q . You interact with senior stakeholders. If you identify a high-risk vulnerability and the stakeholder says 
there's no budget to fix it, what will you do?

Q . Suppose an application is using TLS 1.1. Why is that considered a vulnerability?

Q . How would you convince the business to upgrade from TLS 1.1 to TLS 1.2/1.3?

Q . What would be your overall approach to handling this security risk?

Q . What if the application is legacy and doesn't support TLS 1.2 — what would you do?

Q . If designing a new interface between two applications via APIs, what security measures would you 
implement?
